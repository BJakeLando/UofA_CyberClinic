Privacy

Last updated 2 October 2026

Cyber Squad is a game that teaches children how to spot online tricks. It is built so that it does not need to know who your child is, and it does not ask.

What we never ask for

No name. No email address. No phone number. No login or password. No photograph, video or voice recording. No home address or location. There is nowhere in this game for a child to type any of those things.

Children do not choose their own nickname either. They pick an animal picture and the game assigns a name like SilverPenguin673. This is on purpose: a name a child picks often contains their real one.

What the game does store

  • The nickname the game assigned, and the animal picture chosen
  • Points earned, and which rounds have been played and how many times
  • The room code, if a teacher set up a room for the class
  • The date the nickname was made and when it was last used

None of this is connected to a child's real identity anywhere in our records. A nickname cannot be used to contact anyone.

The game also stores one cookie in the browser. It only keeps track of which game is in progress, so a child does not lose their place. It is not used for advertising and it does not follow anyone to other websites.

The room name

Teachers who set up a room can give it a name, and that name is shown to the class. Please do not put a student's name in it. "Room 12 — 3rd grade" is ideal. This is the only place in the whole game where anyone can type free text.

Who else sees it

Nobody. We do not sell, rent or share this information. There is no advertising in the game and no analytics or tracking services. No part of a page a child loads is fetched from another company.

The game runs on a hosting provider, which keeps the ordinary server records that every website keeps, including the internet address a request came from. Those records are used to keep the service running and for nothing else.

The leaderboard

A leaderboard only ever shows the children in that one room, and only their assigned nicknames and points. A child playing on their own is the only person on their own leaderboard. There is no global or public board.

What children cannot do here

There is no chat, no messaging and no way for one child to contact another. Nothing a child does in this game is visible outside their own classroom's room. There is no way to upload anything, and no links out to other websites.

How long it is kept, and how to have it removed

A room and its scores are kept while the class is using them, and are removed on request. A parent, teacher or school can ask us to delete a room and everything recorded in it, and we will do so. Because we do not record who any nickname belongs to, a request needs the room code.

Schools

This game is intended to be used in class, with a teacher present. If your school needs this in writing for its own privacy review, please get in touch and we will provide it.
